Pokémon is a popular media franchise created by Satoshi Tajiri in 1996. The franchise includes video games, anime, manga, and trading card games. The name 'Pokémon' is a portmanteau of 'Pocket' and 'Monster.' In the Pokémon universe, humans known as trainers catch and train creatures called Pokémon to battle against other trainers. The franchise has become a cultural phenomenon, inspiring a dedicated fan base worldwide. Pokémon games are developed and published by Game Freak and Nintendo, with the first games, Pokémon Red and Green, released in Japan in 1996. The franchise has since grown to include over 20 games across various Nintendo consoles, as well as a successful anime series and trading card game. Pokémon has also expanded into other media, such as movies and books, cementing its place as one of the most beloved franchises of all time.
