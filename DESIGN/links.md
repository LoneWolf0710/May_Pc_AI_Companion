## Design Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- ❌ Cyan neon everywhere on pure #000000 black
- ❌ Rounded bubble chat UIs that look like every other AI
- ❌ Fake data, lorem ipsum, meaningless metric bars
- ❌ Cluttered grids of cards with no hierarchy
- ❌ Static glow effects with no motion design
- ❌ Standard system fonts (use monospace + geometric sans instead)

## Design Principles to Follow

- ✅ Deliberate negative space — let content breathe
- ✅ Desaturated, muted accent colors (dusky blues, soft teals, warm grays)
- ✅ Monospaced fonts for data, geometric sans for headings
- ✅ Physics-based micro-interactions that follow the cursor
- ✅ Layered depth — some elements blurred, some sharp, some high opacity
- ✅ Texture and grain that breaks the "perfect/clinical" AI look
- ✅ Real contextual data labels, not placeholder text
- ✅ Strict grid adherence with intentional breaking
